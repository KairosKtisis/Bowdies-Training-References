Sell It
The house take on a classic — Buffalo Trace muddled with rum-soaked cherries, orange, and bitters over a large cube. Familiar enough to be safe, distinct enough to be memorable.
Staff Note
Buffalo Trace brings vanilla and caramel; the rum-infused cherries set it apart. The brûléed orange adds faint smoke on the nose. Lead with this for spirit-forward but approachable.
Ingredients
  • 2 oz Buffalo Trace
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 3 Rum-infused Cherries
  • 1 Orange Slice
  • 3 dash Angostura Bitters
Method
  1. Muddle orange, sugar, cherries, and bitters in the glass
  2. Add 2 oz Buffalo Trace
  3. Add a large cube and stir
Garnish: Brûléed Orange

Sell It
The benchmark whiskey cocktail — rye, sweet vermouth, and bitters, stirred silky and served with a brandied cherry. Timeless and confident.
Ingredients
  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Sweet Vermouth
  • 2 dash Angostura Bitters
Method
  1. Add all to a mixing glass with ice
  2. Stir until well chilled
  3. Strain over a large cube
Garnish: Brandied Cherry
