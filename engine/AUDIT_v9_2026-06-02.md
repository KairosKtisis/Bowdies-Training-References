## Conventions locked in (cumulative across Phases 1-9)

### File-write protocol
1. Write modified files to sandbox (`/sessions/<id>/mnt/outputs/`)
2. `cp sandbox/file engine/file` for atomic kernel-level copy
3. Never use Write tool directly on `pairing-notes.js` (OneDrive truncation risk)
4. Back up before every modification

### Hash protocol
- For any pair-keyed sweep, hash on `[a, b].sort().join('|')` (canonical sorted key)
- Prevents A|B vs B|A mirror desync
- Applied successfully in Sessions 19, 20, 21, 22 (0 desync × 4 sessions in a row)

### Pre-commit factual scans
- Cooking-method drift (steaks flame-grilled, scallops seared, tuna seared rare)
- Dessert ingredient canon (carrot cake = cream cheese + cinnamon, no raisins/nuts)
- Steak weight canon (10/14/18/26/36/40 oz)
- Bottle facts (proof, age statement, region, distillery character)

### Variant authoring discipline
- Per-bottle variants must reference specific distillery/house facts (Spanish-oak
  Macallan, Côte-des-Blancs Pierre Gimonnet, Sistema-Solera Zacapa)
- Class-level variants must be class-broad but textured (don't invent specific
  distilleries when the variant could apply to dozens of bottles)
- AVOID reasoning must include why + alternative recommendation

### Mandate discipline
- When Gabe sets an absolute mandate ("as good as possible"), don't re-grade it
  mid-project based on visible-corpus-delta or diminishing-returns reasoning
- Surface findings as one-line observations; continue executing toward the goal
